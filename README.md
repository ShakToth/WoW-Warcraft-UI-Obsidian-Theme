# WoW - Warcraft UI — Theme for Obsidian

> *Bring the legendary fantasy MMORPG interface and questlog experience to your personal Obsidian vault.*

**WoW - Warcraft UI** is a dual-mode fantasy theme for Obsidian engineered for adventurers, worldbuilders, game masters, and lorekeepers. It transforms your vault into a living fantasy interface: a dark, atmospheric dungeon & raid console by night, and an authentic aged quest parchment by day.

---

## ⚔️ Dual Mode Experience

### 🌙 Dark Mode: The Dungeon & Tooltip Interface
- **Atmosphere:** Deep dark slate & iron stone (`#111217`, `#161820`) inspired by ancient dungeons, raids, and in-game tooltips.
- **Accents:** Authentic Blizzard Quest Gold (`#f8b700`), aged brass borders (`#c89b3c`), and soft ambient backlighting.
- **Headings & Hierarchy:** High-contrast golden headlines (`Cinzel` & `MedievalSharp`) with bone and ash silver subheadings.
- **Readability:** Gentle off-white bone text (`#ded7cb`) eliminating eye strain during long writing sessions.

### 📜 Light Mode: The Questlog & Spellbook
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, aged vellum and parchment (`#f5ecd5`, `#ebdcc0`) reminiscent of turning pages in your quest log or spellbook.
- **Typography:** Antique iron gall ink typography (`#241a12`) rendered in readable manuscript serif (`Crimson Text`).
- **Accents:** Wax seal crimson (`#8c1b1b`) and antique burnished gold (`#8a5e12`).

## 💎 Features & Custom Elements

### 1. Item Quality Rarity Palette
Tags and links with rarity keywords automatically inherit their iconic glowing Blizzard quality colors:
- ⚪ **Common:** `#ffffff`
- 🟢 **Uncommon:** `#1eff00` (`#uncommon`)
- 🔵 **Rare:** `#0070dd` (`#rare`)
- 🟣 **Epic:** `#a335ee` (`#epic`)
- 🟠 **Legendary:** `#ff8000` (`#legendary`)
- 🟡 **Artifact:** `#e6cc80` (`#artifact`)

### 2. Custom Callouts
Use specialized markdown callouts to structure your campaign notes, quests, and session prep:
- `> [!quest] Objective Title` — Golden quest exclamation border.
- `> [!loot] Item Drop` — Legendary orange loot drop frame.
- `> [!boss] Encounter Name` — Blood-red skull dungeon boss frame.
- `> [!alliance] For the Alliance` — Royal sapphire blue & gold lion banner.
- `> [!horde] For the Horde` — Blood-red & spiked iron banner.
- `> [!rare]`, `> [!epic]`, `> [!legendary]` — Rarity-themed item cards.

### 3. Beveled Tables & UI
- Tables feature gold-tinted headers and alternating slate/parchment row stripes.
- Active tabs feature a signature gold top accent bar.
- Floating menus and modals are framed with metallic brass borders and soft shadows.

## 📦 Installation

### From Obsidian Community Themes (Recommended)
1. Open Obsidian **Settings** (`Ctrl + ,`).
2. Go to **Appearance** ➔ **Themes** ➔ **Manage**.
3. Search for **`WoW - Warcraft UI`**.
4. Click **Install and use**.

### Manual Installation
1. Download `theme.css` and `manifest.json` from the [latest release](https://github.com/ShakToth/WoW-Warcraft-UI-Obsidian-Theme/releases).
2. Inside your vault, go to `.obsidian/themes/`.
3. Create a folder named `WoW - Warcraft UI` and copy both files into it.
4. Open **Settings** ➔ **Appearance** ➔ **Themes** and select **WoW - Warcraft UI**.
